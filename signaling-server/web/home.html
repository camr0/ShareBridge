Features

⚡ Direct P2P Transfer

WebRTC DataChannel for direct device-to-device transfer with no server bottlenecks

🔄 Relay Mode

Automatic fallback when direct connection fails. Your agent's IP stays hidden from recipients.

☁️ Private Cloud Native

Works seamlessly with OpenCloud, Nextcloud, and any WebDAV-compatible storage

👤 No Account Needed

Recipients can join shares with just a link — no registration required

🔐 End-to-End Security

Passwords validated on your agent only — signaling server never sees your secrets

🏠 Self-Hosted

Agent runs on your server — you control your data, your keys, your infrastructure

📖 Open Source

GPLv3 licensed. Audit the code, contribute, or fork it for your needs
